# Novix Community Guidelines
*Version 0.1-draft. Last updated: [November 4, 2026].*

The Novix community is for asking questions, sharing ideas and helping each other. Members can be as young as 15, so we keep it safe and friendly. By posting, you agree to these guidelines and to the User Agreement.

## 1. Be kind
- Treat people with respect. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- No harassment, bullying, hate, or threats.

## 2. Keep people safe
- Do not share your own or anyone else's personal information, such as home address, phone number, school, passwords or private messages.
- Never post your Signature Token or anyone's login details.
- Do not ask other members for personal information or to talk privately somewhere else.
- If someone makes you uncomfortable, use the Report button. If you or someone else is in danger, contact local emergency services right away.

## 3. Stay legal
- No illegal content, no pirated software, and nothing that attacks or damages other people's devices or accounts.
- No sexual content. No content that is violent or graphic.
- Do not share or ask for ways to bypass Novix licensing or activation.

## 4. Keep it useful
- Post in the right category and use a clear title.
- No spam, advertising or repeated posts.
- Posts are text only. Do not post links to untrusted sites.

## 5. Reporting and moderation
Any member can report a post. Moderators may remove posts, lock threads, and pause or end a member's posting. We may act without warning when someone is at risk. Moderator actions are logged. To appeal a decision, contact [novix.center@outlook.com].

## 6. Your posts
You keep ownership of what you write. By posting, you let Novix show it in the community and keep a record of it, including after you edit or delete it, where needed for safety or legal reasons. Only post what you have the right to share.

## 7. Contact
Questions or urgent reports: [novix.center.outlook.com].
