# Novix Privacy Policy
*Version 0.2-draft. Last updated: [DATE].*

This policy explains what personal information [Novix Center] ("Novix", "we", "us") collects when you use the Novix Imager, Novix OS and this website, and what we do with it. Questions: [novix.center@outlook.com].

## 1. What we collect
- **Account details:** your email address, your legal name (kept private: it is never shown in the community), your date of birth (used to check the minimum age) and your password, which is handled by our sign-in provider and never stored in readable form.
- **Optional details:** a profile picture, if you upload one. If you verify a phone number for adult verification, we also keep that phone number.
- **Community:** the community name you choose, the posts and replies you write, reports you make, and, if you choose to show it, your profile picture. These are visible to other signed-in members. We also keep a record of moderation actions.
- **Purchases and licenses:** which editions you own, your Signature Tokens, and whether a payment succeeded or was refunded. Card details are handled by [PAYMENT PROCESSOR]. We do not see or store your full card number.
- **Agreements:** which version of each agreement you accepted, and when.
- **Adult verification:** the date, the method used and the outcome. We do not keep copies of identity documents.
- **Security records:** a log of important actions on accounts, such as approvals, so we can investigate problems.
- **Activation (when available):** an identifier derived from your device's hardware, so a license can be tied to the device it was activated on.

## 2. How we use it
To create and run your account, sell and deliver licenses, check minimum age and adult verification, prevent fraud and abuse, give support, keep records the law requires, and improve Novix. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertising.

## 3. Who we share it with
Only companies that help us run Novix, and only what they need:
- **Supabase**, for our database, sign-in and private file storage.
- **[PAYMENT PROCESSOR]**, to take payments.
- **[SMS PROVIDER]**, to send phone verification codes.
- **GitHub**, which hosts this website and the Imager downloads.

We may also share information when the law requires it, or as part of a sale of our business, and we will tell you if that happens.

## 4. Children
Novix accounts are for people aged 15 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone younger. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. A parent or guardian who believes a child has given us information can contact [novix.center@outlook.com]. Schools and organizations that manage devices are covered by a separate agreement with us: [ORGANIZATION DATA TERMS].

## 5. How long we keep it
We keep account information while your account is open. We keep purchase, license and agreement records for as long as the law and our accounting need them [RETENTION PERIOD]. Security logs are kept for [LOG RETENTION].

## 6. Security
We use reputable providers, access controls and encrypted connections, and we limit who can see personal information. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

## 7. Your choices
You can see and update your name, email, password and profile picture in the Imager's Account settings. To ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or to close your account and delete your information, contact [novix.center@outlook.com]. We may need to keep some records, such as purchases, where the law requires it. You may also have rights under the privacy laws where you live, and you can contact the regulator in your area.

## 8. This website
This website does not use cookies or advertising trackers. GitHub, which hosts it, may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit, under its own policy.
